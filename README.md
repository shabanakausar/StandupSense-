# StandupSense-
AI Chief of Staff for small teams — pulls real Notion and GitHub  activity, detects blockers deterministically, and uses IBM Granite  to surface the one decision your team actually needs to make today.
StandupSense is an AI Chief of Staff for small teams. It connects directly to Notion and GitHub — not self-reported status — to detect real blockers using 
deterministic rules, then uses IBM Granite to write an honest summary and surface the single most important decision your team needs to resolve today.
Built end-to-end with IBM Bob for the AI Builders Challenge (Wild Card theme).
IBM Granite models ibm/granite-4-h-small and IBM BOb designed this.
